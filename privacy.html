STL2STP Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

Bo Zhang / ZizWorks provides STL2STP for iPhone and iPad. The app does not require an account, send your models or app usage to us, use advertising or analytics SDKs, or track you across apps or websites.

Files and conversion

STL import, preview and conversion run on your device. The app may keep local working copies, conversion results and history, and settings. When you select a cloud location in Files, export, share, or enable system backups, the destination or backup service you choose handles its own copy. We do not automatically upload your models or results.

Purchases and internet access

Conversion can run without an internet connection once the source file is available on the device. The App Store is contacted through Apple StoreKit to load products, process optional tips and the one-time Pro purchase, and restore Pro. Apple handles payment and authentication. We do not receive payment card details or send receipts to a developer server. The app checks purchase entitlements on the device and caches the Pro status locally. Tips grant no features or credits.

Opening the privacy or support link uses your browser and requires internet access. Apple explains its own handling of App Store data in its App Store privacy notice.

Deleting data and getting help

You can clear conversion history in Settings or delete the app to remove its local app data. Copies you exported or backed up are managed in their respective destinations. The support site uses GitHub Pages, which logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. We add no site analytics or tracking. See GitHub Pages’ data collection information.

If you email us, we receive your address and what you choose to send. We use that information to respond and retain it only for as long as necessary to resolve and document the request or meet applicable obligations. For questions or to request deletion of support correspondence, contact 1363555264@qq.com. Updates to this policy will be published here.

STL2STP 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 24 日

Bo Zhang / ZizWorks 提供 STL2STP iPhone 和 iPad 版。App 无需注册，也不会向开发者发送模型或使用记录。App 不使用广告或分析 SDK，不跨应用或网站追踪你。

文件与转换

STL 导入、预览和转换在设备上完成。App 可能在本机保存工作副本、转换结果、历史记录及设置。如果你在「文件」里选择云端位置、主动导出或分享，或开启系统备份，所选目的地或备份服务会处理相应副本。我们不会自动上传模型或转换结果。

内购与联网

源文件已在设备上时，转换可以离线进行。加载商品、自愿打赏、一次性购买 Pro 或恢复 Pro 时，会通过 Apple StoreKit 连接 App Store。Apple 处理付款和身份验证。我们不接收付款卡信息，也不会向开发者服务器发送交易回执。App 在本机核验购买权益并缓存 Pro 状态。打赏不提供功能或点数。

打开政策页或支持页时会使用浏览器，需要联网。Apple 如何处理 App Store 数据，请查看 Apple 的 App Store 隐私说明。

删除数据与联系

你可以在设置中清除转换历史，也可删除 App 以移除其本机数据。已导出或备份的副本请在对应位置管理。支持站由 GitHub Pages 托管，GitHub 出于安全目的记录访客 IP。我们未添加网站分析或追踪。详见 GitHub Pages 数据收集说明。

如果你主动发邮件，我们会收到邮箱和你选择提供的内容。信息用于回应请求，仅在处理、记录请求或履行适用义务所需的期限内保存。如需咨询或申请删除客服往来，请联系 1363555264@qq.com。政策更新会在本页公布。
